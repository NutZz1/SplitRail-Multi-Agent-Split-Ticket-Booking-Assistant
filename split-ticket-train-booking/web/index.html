A THOUGHTFUL WAY TO COMPARE

One search. Four perspectives.

  1. Explore the same train

    Find direct tickets and options that switch coaches at eligible stops.

  2. Look for connections

    A second search explores journeys across different trains.

  3. Consider comfort and cost

    Two evaluators assess coach distance, night transfers, estimated fares, and journey time.

  4. Bring it all together

    The coordinator removes duplicates, checks constraints, and ranks options using a weighted score. You can sort and filter the results.

Recommended balances moving time, estimated fare, transfer penalty, and layover. It is not a guarantee of the cheapest possible fare. Same-train searches currently allow at most one coach change.
